Bio

I am a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh, where I study AI model compression through structured linear algebra. Before that I completed the joint Computational Science and Engineering master’s programme at KTH and TU Delft, graduating cum laude.

My research explores questions such as: Can we use structured matrices to reduce the cost of attention? What can linear attention learn from numerical linear algebra? How compactly can we store the KV cache? And how can we turn these ideas into efficient GPU implementations?
