What I work on

Research

I am a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh, where I study AI model compression through structured linear algebra. I completed the joint Computational Science and Engineering master’s programme at KTH and TU Delft, graduating cum laude.

My research explores questions such as: Can we exploit tensor decompositions or structured operators to reduce the memory footprint of neural networks? What are the trade-offs between memory usage, computational cost, and model accuracy? And how can we translate these mathematical ideas into efficient GPU implementations?
